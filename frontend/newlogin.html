About Us

Who we are.

There’s nothing cookie-cutter about Mad Food. Not our kitchens. Not our menu. And definitely not the way we live life. Around here, we don’t settle for anything less than food we’re proud to serve. And we don’t just clock in. Not when we can also become our best, make friends, and have fun while we’re at it. We’re the food company that lives life unboxed.

What we’re about.

At Mad Food, we don’t just make food. We make people happy. Mad Food was built on the belief that every partner experience should be special, and we carry that belief into everything we do. With more than years of experience under our belts, we understand how to best serve our customers through tried and true service principles: We create food we’re proud to serve and deliver it fast, with a smile.

Where we come from.

In 1958, two brothers borrowed $600 from their mom to open a food place. They named it Mad Food because their sign only had room for eight letters. How profound! Today we keep building on that same energy: bold ideas, fast service, and a partner community that runs on trust and flavor.
